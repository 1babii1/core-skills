# Closeout, Referrals, and Repeat Sales

## Closeout sequence

Complete the commercial close in evidence-backed order:

1. Verify agreed acceptance evidence and unresolved defects.
2. Record deferred items and new requests separately.
3. Confirm release and operational status through `delivery-core`.
4. Deliver agreed source, documentation, data/export, accounts, and training.
5. Transfer ownership and non-secret access through approved secure mechanisms.
6. Confirm final invoice/payment state without exposing financial credentials.
7. State warranty, support, maintenance, hosting, monitoring, backup, and third-party boundaries.
8. Record known risks, future review dates, and responsible owners.
9. Close or transition the CRM opportunity.
10. Capture estimation, qualification, delivery, and communication lessons.

Do not call a project complete merely because the code exists or production deployment succeeded.

## Outcome review

At an appropriate time after launch, compare:

- promised deliverables and acceptance;
- actual user/business outcome where measurable;
- product and operational signals;
- unresolved friction and support demand;
- estimate versus actual effort and calendar causes;
- client-provided dependencies and decision speed;
- profitability and opportunity cost;
- relationship health and future fit.

Separate delivered work from downstream outcomes outside the developer's control.

## Requesting proof or referral

Ask only after delivered value or a clearly positive engagement signal. Obtain permission for:

- client name and logo;
- quote and attribution;
- screenshots or data;
- measurable results;
- public case study;
- referral introduction.

Offer a draft for correction, but never invent the quote. Respect confidentiality and a refusal without repeated pressure.

## Repeat work

Look for a justified next engagement:

- an observed operational bottleneck;
- deferred scope that now has evidence;
- maintenance or reliability need;
- measured usability, performance, SEO, accessibility, or conversion issue;
- a new workflow that shares validated foundations;
- training, documentation, or safe ownership transfer.

For each opportunity state:

- evidence and client outcome;
- why now;
- smallest useful intervention;
- expected decision or success measure;
- risk and dependency;
- whether it belongs to maintenance, a new project, or a change order.

Do not create dependency by withholding documentation, access, reusable knowledge, or reasonable handoff. Repeat business should come from trust and value.

## Retrospective

Capture:

- acquisition source and time to qualify;
- why the client chose or rejected the offer;
- missing facts that changed the estimate;
- actual effort and elapsed-time variance by cause;
- scope changes and whether the process handled them;
- payment and acceptance friction;
- reusable assets or lessons that do not disclose client information;
- changes to positioning, qualification, estimates, agreements, or delivery.

Use the learning to improve the system, not to rewrite historical facts.
