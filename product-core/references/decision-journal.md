# Decision Journal

Record consequential product decisions in this compact form:

```markdown
# Decision: [title]
Date:
Owner:
Status: proposed | accepted | reversed | superseded
Decision deadline:

## Context and decision
[What must be decided and why now]

## Evidence
- [claim type] [source or artifact]

## Options and trade-offs
1. ...

## Decision and rationale
...

## Assumptions and dissent
...

## Expected result
Metric:
Baseline:
Target/threshold:
Time window:
Guardrails:

## Revisit
Review date:
Evidence that would reverse this decision:
Actual result:
Learning:
```

Link superseding decisions rather than rewriting history. A decision without a review condition becomes an opinion preserved as policy.
