# Product Artifact Review

Review an artifact adversarially without rewriting it first.

## Severity

- **Blocker:** proceeding could build the wrong product, create material legal/ethical harm, or make success impossible to evaluate.
- **Major:** important evidence, scope, acceptance, measurement, or decision gap.
- **Minor:** ambiguity or maintainability issue unlikely to change the central decision.

## Review pillars

1. Decision and owner are explicit.
2. User/segment and circumstance are specific.
3. Claims trace to evidence; assumptions and unknowns are labeled.
4. Contradictory evidence and alternatives are represented.
5. Problem is not disguised solution language.
6. Hypothesis can fail and has a decision rule.
7. Scope includes exclusions, dependencies, and change handling.
8. Requirements and acceptance are observable.
9. Metrics have formulas, populations, windows, baselines/collection plans, targets with rationale, and guardrails.
10. Privacy, security, accessibility, reliability, support, and lifecycle are considered when relevant.
11. Next investment is proportional to evidence.
12. Review date and ship/iterate/stop action are defined.

Report findings with location, consequence, missing evidence, and a concrete correction. Do not mark the artifact valid merely because all headings exist.
