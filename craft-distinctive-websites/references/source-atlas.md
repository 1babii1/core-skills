## Broader collection

`https://tilda.education/web-design-trends-2026` groups further examples around:

- cyberbrutalism and authored digital error;
- AR and spatial interaction;
- interactive 3D as a narrative/product center;
- dimensional UI, physical light, realistic materials, and layered transparency;
- organic tactile forms;
- short reactive motion with one dominant moving object;
- hyperminimalism and designed negative space;
- antidesign for narrow audiences;
- microelements contrasted with macro accents;
- display typography as image, texture, and brand voice.

The collection repeatedly states useful guardrails: justify expensive spatial experiences, keep UX above spectacle, animate a focal object rather than everything, keep motion short enough to be understood, protect readability, and match experimental choices to audience and product.
